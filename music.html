Columns = song progress · rows = sounds

Columns show the song moving from step 1 to 16. Each row is a sound/part; rows play together so sounds can overlap.

Each column is a beat step. The rows are notes from high to low. Hold Shift and tap another step on the same note row to extend a note. Select a note, then use ← / → to shorten or lengthen it. Your saved patterns stay in this browser.
